Privacy & HIPAA Notice

Last updated: June 2026

AWAREmed Health & Wellness Resource Center is committed to protecting the privacy and security of your personal and health information. This notice describes how medical information about you may be used and disclosed, and how you can access this information.

Information We Collect

We may collect personal information including your name, contact information, and health history when you contact us, book a consultation, or use our services. We do not collect sensitive health information through our website without your explicit consent.

HIPAA Compliance

As a healthcare practice, AWAREmed complies with the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA). Your protected health information (PHI) is handled in accordance with HIPAA requirements and applicable state privacy laws.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and manage your healthcare consultations
  • To communicate with you about appointments and care
  • To coordinate care with other providers (with your authorization)
  • To fulfill legal and regulatory obligations

We Do Not Sell Your Information

AWAREmed does not sell, rent, or share your personal or health information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Your Rights

You have the right to access your health information, request corrections, and understand how your information is used. Contact us to exercise these rights.

Website Data

Our website may use standard analytics tools to understand site usage. These tools do not collect personally identifiable health information.

Contact Us

For questions about your privacy rights or to access your health information, contact us at (423) 430-6170 or awaremed@gmail.com. 1604 Lamons Lane, Suite 202, Johnson City, TN 37604.

This notice should be reviewed by qualified legal counsel to ensure compliance with all applicable HIPAA and state privacy regulations.
