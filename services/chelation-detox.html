Overview

What Is Chelation & Detoxification Support?

Chelation therapy uses specific agents, administered orally or intravenously, that bind to heavy metals and other toxins in the bloodstream, enabling them to be safely excreted by the kidneys. It is a well-established medical procedure with decades of use in conventional medicine for heavy metal poisoning, and increasingly studied in integrative medicine for broader applications.

At AWAREmed, chelation and detoxification support is always conducted under the direct supervision of Dr. Dalal Akoury, MD. We never offer detoxification protocols without a thorough clinical evaluation, including detailed health history, relevant lab work, kidney and liver function assessment, and a review of your current medications and health conditions.

Detoxification support at AWAREmed goes beyond chelation. Dr. Akoury addresses the full spectrum of the body's detoxification pathways, liver, kidneys, lymphatic system, gut, and skin, with personalized nutritional, lifestyle, and therapeutic support designed to optimize your body's natural cleansing capacity.

Who May Benefit?

This service may be appropriate for individuals including:

  • Individuals with documented heavy metal toxicity or exposure
  • Those with symptoms potentially related to toxic burden (fatigue, cognitive fog, muscle pain)
  • Patients with occupational exposure to heavy metals or chemicals
  • Those seeking comprehensive wellness and metabolic optimization
  • Individuals with chronic illness who have not responded to conventional care
  • Patients interested in preventive environmental health support

The Treatment Process at AWAREmed

Every chelation and detox protocol begins with a comprehensive evaluation by Dr. Akoury. This includes detailed lab work, heavy metal testing, liver and kidney function panels, inflammatory markers, and nutritional status assessment. Based on results, a personalized protocol is designed that may include IV or oral chelation (as appropriate), nutritional support, liver-supportive therapies, IV nutrient infusions, and lifestyle guidance. All interventions are closely monitored throughout.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is chelation therapy safe?+
Under proper physician supervision with thorough pre-evaluation, chelation is generally well-tolerated. Dr. Akoury assesses kidney function, cardiovascular status, and other relevant factors before any chelation protocol. Close monitoring throughout treatment is standard practice at AWAREmed.
How do I know if I have heavy metal toxicity?+
Heavy metal testing is conducted through specialized lab analysis. Dr. Akoury reviews your symptoms, exposure history, and lab results to determine whether heavy metal burden may be contributing to your health concerns.
How many chelation sessions are needed?+
The number of sessions depends on your heavy metal levels, health status, and response to treatment. Dr. Akoury designs an individualized protocol and reassesses regularly throughout your care.
Is detox support different from chelation?+
Yes. Detoxification support at AWAREmed encompasses a broader range of approaches, including nutritional support for liver detox pathways, gut health optimization, and lifestyle interventions, that complement chelation therapy or stand alone for general detoxification support.
