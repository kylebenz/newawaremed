Overview

What Is Hormone Optimization?

Hormone optimization through Bioidentical Hormone Replacement Therapy (BHRT) addresses the complex interplay of hormones that regulate virtually every system in your body, from energy and sleep to mood, metabolism, libido, and cognitive function. When hormones fall out of balance, the effects can be far-reaching and deeply disruptive to your quality of life.

At AWAREmed, Dr. Dalal Akoury, MD takes a comprehensive, whole-person approach to hormonal health. Rather than simply replacing deficient hormones, she evaluates your complete hormonal ecosystem, thyroid, adrenal, sex hormones, and more, and designs a personalized plan that works with your unique biology.

Bioidentical hormones are structurally identical to those your body naturally produces, which distinguishes them from synthetic hormones used in traditional HRT. Dr. Akoury creates individualized protocols based on thorough lab evaluation, symptom assessment, and your personal health history.

Potential Benefits

While individual responses vary and no specific outcomes can be guaranteed, this service may support:

Improved energy levels and reduced fatigue

Restored libido and sexual health
Healthier body composition and metabolism
Reduced symptoms of perimenopause and menopause
Improved bone density and cardiovascular markers
Better stress response and adrenal balance
Greater overall sense of vitality and well-being

Who May Benefit?

This service may be appropriate for individuals including:

  • Women experiencing perimenopause or menopause symptoms
  • Men with low testosterone (andropause)
  • Individuals with thyroid imbalances
  • Anyone experiencing unexplained fatigue, mood changes, or weight gain
  • Those with adrenal dysfunction or chronic stress patterns
  • Patients seeking preventive, age-management medicine

The Treatment Process at AWAREmed

Your hormone optimization journey begins with an in-depth consultation with Dr. Akoury, who reviews your complete health history and current symptoms. Comprehensive hormone labs are ordered, including thyroid panel, sex hormones, adrenal markers, and metabolic indicators. Based on these results, Dr. Akoury designs a personalized BHRT protocol, which may include bioidentical hormone preparations in oral, topical, injectable, or pellet form. Follow-up labs and consultations ensure your protocol is refined over time for optimal results.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long before I notice results?+
Many patients notice initial improvements within 4, 8 weeks, though full benefits typically develop over 3, 6 months. Dr. Akoury monitors your progress with follow-up labs and adjusts your protocol accordingly.
Are bioidentical hormones safe?+
Bioidentical hormones carry their own risk and benefit profile. Dr. Akoury conducts a thorough medical evaluation before recommending BHRT and monitors each patient closely. Safety is always the primary consideration.
Is BHRT only for women?+
No. Men also benefit significantly from hormone optimization, particularly testosterone optimization, thyroid support, and adrenal balance as part of comprehensive men's health care.
How is BHRT different from conventional HRT?+
Bioidentical hormones are molecularly identical to those your body produces naturally. Dr. Akoury also focuses on identifying and addressing the root causes of imbalance rather than simply managing symptoms.
