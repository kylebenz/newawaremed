Overview

What Is Brain & Cognitive Wellness?

Cognitive health is influenced by a complex web of factors, hormones, nutrients, inflammation, gut health, sleep, toxin burden, vascular health, and genetics. When any of these systems falls out of balance, the effects on brain function can be profound: difficulty concentrating, memory lapses, mood disturbances, anxiety, depression, and fatigue are among the most common complaints Dr. Akoury hears from patients.

At AWAREmed, brain and cognitive wellness is approached through the lens of functional medicine, identifying the root drivers of cognitive dysfunction rather than simply managing symptoms. Dr. Dalal Akoury, MD conducts a comprehensive evaluation of every system that influences brain health, from thyroid and sex hormones to gut function, vascular health, nutritional status, and sleep quality.

The result is a personalized, multi-dimensional protocol that addresses your cognitive concerns from every relevant angle, supporting both immediate function and long-term brain resilience.

Who May Benefit?

This service may be appropriate for individuals including:

  • Those experiencing brain fog, poor focus, or mental fatigue
  • Individuals with memory concerns or cognitive decline risk
  • Patients with mood disturbances (anxiety, depression, irritability)
  • Those with sleep disorders affecting cognitive function
  • Individuals with hormonal imbalances affecting brain health
  • Anyone seeking preventive strategies for long-term brain health
  • Patients with autoimmune conditions and neurological involvement

The Treatment Process at AWAREmed

Brain wellness care at AWAREmed begins with an extensive clinical consultation covering your cognitive symptoms, medical history, lifestyle, sleep patterns, stress levels, and family history. Functional labs assess hormonal balance, nutritional status, inflammatory markers, thyroid function, and gut health, all systems that significantly impact brain function. Dr. Akoury designs a personalized protocol that may include hormonal optimization, targeted nutritional therapies, IV nutrient support, lifestyle medicine, and stress management strategies.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can functional medicine help with anxiety and depression?+
Dr. Akoury takes a root-cause approach to mood disturbances, evaluating underlying nutritional, hormonal, inflammatory, and gut-related factors that may be contributing. This is supportive care, we always recommend working with a mental health professional for diagnosed mood disorders.
What role does nutrition play in brain health?+
Nutrition profoundly affects brain function. Deficiencies in B vitamins, omega-3 fatty acids, magnesium, zinc, and other nutrients directly impact neurotransmitter production, neuroinflammation, and cognitive function. Dr. Akoury identifies and addresses these through targeted testing and supplementation.
Is cognitive decline reversible?+
Many factors contributing to cognitive decline are modifiable when identified early. Dr. Akoury focuses on optimizing all relevant systems to support the best possible brain function, though individual results vary significantly.
Can IV therapy support brain health?+
Certain IV formulations, including NAD+, glutathione, B vitamins, and high-dose Vitamin C, may support neurological health and function. Dr. Akoury evaluates whether IV therapy is appropriate as part of your brain wellness protocol.
