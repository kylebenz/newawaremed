Overview

What Is IV Therapy?

Intravenous (IV) therapy delivers fluids, vitamins, minerals, amino acids, and other nutrients directly into the bloodstream, bypassing the digestive system for immediate, highly efficient absorption. Unlike oral supplements, IV therapy ensures that nutrients reach your cells at levels that simply cannot be achieved through the gut alone.

At AWAREmed, every IV therapy protocol is designed and supervised by Dr. Dalal Akoury, MD. We never use a one-size-fits-all "drip menu." Instead, your infusion is built around your specific health history, functional lab results, symptoms, and wellness goals.

How IV Therapy Works

During your session, a small intravenous catheter is placed, typically in the arm, and the customized nutrient solution is infused slowly over 30 to 90 minutes, depending on the formulation. Most patients find the experience relaxing. You may read, rest, or use your phone during the infusion.

The nutrients enter your bloodstream directly, bypassing digestive absorption limitations. This is particularly valuable for patients with gut absorption issues, chronic illness, high-performance recovery needs, or those requiring therapeutic nutrient concentrations that oral supplementation cannot achieve.

Antioxidant support and reduction of oxidative stress
Nutritional support for those with malabsorption conditions
Supportive care during cancer treatment (integrative oncology)

Who May Benefit From IV Therapy?

IV therapy may be appropriate for individuals experiencing:

  • Chronic fatigue or low energy levels
  • Frequent illness or weakened immunity
  • Dehydration from illness, travel, or exertion
  • Nutritional deficiencies or malabsorption
  • Cognitive fog or difficulty concentrating
  • Recovery from athletic performance or physical stress
  • Ongoing support during cancer care
  • Stress management and adrenal support
  • Migraine or headache patterns
  • General wellness and preventive health optimization

What to Expect at Your IV Therapy Visit

Your first IV therapy session at AWAREmed begins with a clinical consultation with Dr. Akoury. She will review your health history, current symptoms, medications, and functional lab results (if available) to determine the most appropriate formulation for you.

During the infusion, you'll relax in a comfortable setting. Our team monitors you throughout. Most infusions take 30, 90 minutes. After your session, you may notice effects immediately or within a day or two, though responses vary significantly between individuals.

Safety and Physician Supervision

At AWAREmed, IV therapy is never administered without proper clinical evaluation. Dr. Akoury personally assesses each patient before recommending IV treatment. Contraindications, allergies, kidney function, and cardiac status are always considered. Your safety is our highest priority.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is IV therapy painful?+
The initial needle insertion may cause brief discomfort, similar to a standard blood draw. Once the catheter is placed, most patients feel no pain and find the infusion relaxing.
How often should I receive IV therapy?+
Frequency varies based on your health goals. Some patients benefit from weekly sessions; others do well with monthly maintenance. Dr. Akoury will recommend a schedule based on your individual needs and response.
Are there risks or side effects?+
IV therapy is generally well-tolerated under physician supervision. Possible side effects include temporary discomfort at the insertion site, a cool or warm sensation during infusion, or very rarely, bruising. Serious adverse events are uncommon when properly evaluated and monitored.
Does insurance cover IV therapy?+
Coverage varies significantly by insurer and formulation. Please contact our office directly to discuss your specific situation. We are happy to help clarify what options are available to you.
