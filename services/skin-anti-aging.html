Overview

What Is Integrative Skin & Anti-Aging Medicine?

True skin health is a reflection of overall health. While topical treatments can improve skin appearance, lasting, meaningful improvement requires addressing what is happening inside, hormonal balance, nutritional status, inflammation, gut health, oxidative stress, and cellular aging. At AWAREmed, skin and anti-aging care begins from the inside out.

Dr. Dalal Akoury, MD takes a comprehensive, integrative approach to skin health and anti-aging that goes far beyond topical solutions. She evaluates the internal factors driving skin aging, including hormonal decline, nutritional deficiencies, chronic inflammation, gut dysbiosis, and toxic burden, and addresses them with targeted therapeutic protocols while supporting the skin's external environment.

The result is a personalized approach that supports healthy, radiant skin as a natural expression of optimized inner health, complemented by evidence-informed aesthetic support where appropriate.

Potential Benefits

While individual responses vary and no specific outcomes can be guaranteed, this service may support:

Improved skin texture, tone, and elasticity
Reduced appearance of fine lines and wrinkles

Addressed skin conditions (acne, rosacea, eczema, psoriasis)
Hormonal balance supporting skin health
Nutritional optimization for collagen production
Reduced systemic inflammation
Antioxidant protection and reduced oxidative stress
Improved hydration and skin barrier function
Enhanced confidence and well-being

Who May Benefit?

This service may be appropriate for individuals including:

  • Those experiencing skin aging, wrinkles, or loss of elasticity
  • Individuals with chronic skin conditions (acne, rosacea, eczema)
  • Those with hormonal skin changes (perimenopause, post-partum)
  • Patients interested in preventive anti-aging strategies
  • Anyone seeking a root-cause approach to skin health
  • Individuals who have tried topical treatments without lasting results

The Treatment Process at AWAREmed

Dr. Akoury begins with a comprehensive consultation assessing your skin concerns, overall health history, hormonal status, nutritional patterns, gut health, and stress levels. Relevant lab work may be ordered, including hormone panels, nutritional markers, inflammatory markers, and gut function. A personalized anti-aging protocol is designed addressing internal root causes while complementing with targeted external and aesthetic support appropriate for your situation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does AWAREmed offer aesthetic procedures?+
Dr. Akoury's approach to skin health is primarily internal, addressing root causes through hormonal, nutritional, and functional medicine. She can discuss whether aesthetic procedures may complement your internal protocol and provide appropriate referrals when indicated.
How does hormone balance affect skin?+
Estrogen, testosterone, thyroid hormones, and cortisol all significantly affect skin thickness, moisture, collagen production, and aging. Dr. Akoury evaluates and optimizes hormonal balance as a key component of skin health.
Can gut health really affect skin?+
Yes, significantly. The gut-skin axis is well-established. Dysbiosis, intestinal permeability, and chronic gut inflammation drive systemic inflammation that manifests in skin conditions. Dr. Akoury addresses gut health as part of comprehensive skin and anti-aging care.
How quickly will I see results?+
Internal anti-aging protocols produce gradual, sustainable improvements over weeks to months. Many patients notice improvements in skin hydration, tone, and texture within 4, 8 weeks; deeper changes in collagen support and aging patterns develop over longer periods.
