About AWAREmed Health & Wellness Resource Center

A physician-led integrative and functional medicine practice in Johnson City, Tennessee, built on the belief that true health requires caring for the whole person.

AWAREmed
Johnson City, TN
Our Mission

Whole-Person Care, Rooted in Listening

AWAREmed Health & Wellness Resource Center was founded by Dr. Dalal Akoury, MD to offer a different kind of care, one that treats people as whole human beings rather than a list of symptoms or lab values.

We combine conventional medical understanding with integrative, functional, and regenerative approaches, always personalized to the individual, always honest, and always supportive of your existing care relationships.

Our mission is simple: to support your health across body, mind, and spirit, with compassion, education, and genuine respect for your goals and your story.

We Listen

Appointments are unhurried. Your full history matters, and so does how you feel.

We Personalize

Every plan is built around your biology, history, and goals, never a one-size-fits-all template.

We Coordinate

Integrative care works best alongside your existing providers. We support, not replace, conventional care.

We Educate

You leave every visit understanding your options, with realistic expectations and honest guidance.

What Sets Us Apart

How We Practice

Six principles that guide every patient relationship at AWAREmed.

️

Root-Cause Focus

We look beyond symptoms to understand the underlying drivers, hormonal, nutritional, environmental, and more.

Whole-Person Philosophy

Body, mind, and spirit are deeply connected. Every aspect of your health is considered in your care plan.

Evidence-Informed

Our integrative approaches are rooted in science and clinical experience, not trends or unproven claims.

Globally Accessible

Dr. Akoury serves patients in person in Johnson City and virtually worldwide in four languages.

️

Responsible Practice

We follow applicable regulations, present only accurate information, and prioritize your safety above all else.

Transparent Communication

You always know what we recommend, why we recommend it, and what the realistic expectations are.
