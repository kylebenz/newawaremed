About AWAREmed Health & Wellness Resource Center

Quality Healthcare, Inside & Out. A physician-led integrative and functional medicine practice in Johnson City, Tennessee, with 40+ years of clinical experience and a mission to support your health at every level, body, mind, and spirit.

AWAREmed

Our Mission

Quality Healthcare, Inside & Out

AWAREmed Health & Wellness Resource Center was founded by Dr. Dalal Akoury, MD, an integrative physician with more than 40 years of clinical experience, fellowship training in Pediatric Hematology/Oncology at St. Jude Children's Research Hospital and Emory University, and NIH-funded research in leukemia and PCR diagnostics.

We combine conventional medical understanding with integrative, functional, and regenerative approaches, always personalized to the individual, always honest, and always supportive of your existing care relationships.

"Balanced health inside and out is our primary mission. Youthfulness and health are the foundations of a good quality of life, and we are here to support you on every step of that journey." — AWAREmed Health and Wellness Resource Center

Our Roadmap

The AWAREmed 4-Phase Wellness Roadmap

A structured, physician-guided path toward whole-person well-being. Results vary based on individual health status.

01

Review & Comprehensive Assessment

In-depth evaluation of your health history, lifestyle, and key biomarkers including antioxidant levels, hormone panels, gut microbiome, and more, to build your personalized wellness picture.

02

Digestive & Gut Wellness Support

Targeted support for gut microbiome balance, digestive function, and nutritional absorption as foundational pillars of overall health and immune function.

03

Nervous System & Hormone Support

Physician-supervised programs addressing neurotransmitter balance, bio-identical hormone optimization, and stress-resilience for whole-body harmony.

04

Cellular Balance & Environmental Awareness

Advanced support for detoxification pathways, oxidative stress reduction, and environmental health awareness to promote cellular vitality and longevity.

Signature Programs

Comprehensive Wellness Programs at Our Johnson City Clinic

Each program is developed collaboratively between physician and patient. Results vary based on individual health status, adherence, and other factors.

01

Hormone Balance & Bio-Identical Hormone Support

Physician-supervised hormone education for individuals experiencing changes in energy, metabolism, sleep, or mood. Bio-identical hormone options discussed where clinically appropriate. Does not diagnose or treat hormonal disorders.

02

Weight Management & Metabolic Wellness

A multidisciplinary, physician-guided program analyzing factors that may contribute to weight challenges through individualized nutrition, movement, and lifestyle planning. No specific weight loss outcomes are guaranteed.

03

Lifestyle Assessment & Modification Program

Individualized diet and exercise planning, stress-reduction education, and mental health support resources. Assessments scheduled every four to six months to evaluate progress and adjust recommendations.

04

Aesthetic & Anti-Aging Wellness

Supportive aesthetic services in AWAREmed's serene facility under physician oversight. Intended to complement overall wellness, not to replace dermatological or surgical care.

05

Raw Food Juice Bar & Apothecary

On-site nutritional support options as part of a comprehensive lifestyle program. Not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease.
